Networks installed within small offices / home are known as Small-Office / Home-Office (SOHO) they allow users on small networks to share resources such as printers

Business and SOHO networks provide a shared connection to the internet. The internet can be referred to as a "network of networks"

Types of local networks
	- Small Home Network
	- Small office / home office
	- Medium to large networks
	- world wide networks (internet)

Types of mobile devices
	- Smartphones
	- Tablets
	- Smartwatches
	- Smart Glasses

Connected Home Devices
	- Security systems
	- Appliances
	- Smart TV
	- Game Consoles

Other Connected Devices
	- Medical devices
	- smart cars
	- RFID tags	
	- Sensors and actuators

Bandwidth - The amount of bits that can reach a destination simultaneously.
Throughput - Bandwidth with latency considerations measures in bits per second

Transmission Media Types
	- Optical (fiber optic)
	- Electrical (Copper Cable)
	- Radio Waves (Wi-Fi)
