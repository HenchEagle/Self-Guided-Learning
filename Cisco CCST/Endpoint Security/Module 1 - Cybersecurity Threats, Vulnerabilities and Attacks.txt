Threat Domain - An area of control, authority or protection that attackers can exploit to gain access to systems.

User Domain - anyone with access to an organisations information systems, including employees, contract partners and customers.

Application Domain - All critical systems, applications and data an organisation utilises to support operations. 

Cyber threats are organised into different categories to allow organisations to assess the likelihood and impact of them to be able to prioritise security efforts.
