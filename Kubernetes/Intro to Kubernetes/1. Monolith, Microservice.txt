A monolith is a large application / piece of software often built upon old architectures or outdated programming languages, with
lots of redundant logic that is not designed for portability. These services must be fully ran on a singular process requiring hardware
to match the required compute of the service. This is often expensive and complex. The only real scalability tactic is outwards scaling,
(direct replication of the service on another machine) This is also costly, introduces complexity and requires additional features like
load balancing.
