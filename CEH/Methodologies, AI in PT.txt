Hacking is the process of utilising and understanding a complex system of some form to make it perform in a way that it was not designed to do so.

Advancments in AI have lead to more sophisticated cyber threats as hackers increasingly use Ai driven tools to enhance and automate their attacks presenting significant challenges to cybersecurity

Ai is heavily leveraged within ethical hacking nowadays allowing secuirty professionals to outpace malicious actors, providing benefits
such as: efficiency, accuracy, scalability and cost effectiveness inclucing locally hosted LLMs utilised for pentesting providing
privacy and anonymity as no data is ever sent to the cloud organisation providing the AI model.

shellGPT is a new tool that allows automation of several tasks such as gathering data from a large range of social media, forums, sites etc.
as well as provide real time assistance.



== LLM Capabilities, Ethical Hackers ==
- Accelerating Analysis: LLMs can rapidly analyse large datasets, security logs or network traffic effectively and efficiently
  identifying patters that could indicate breach or vulnerabilities. This enables ethical hackers to quickly detect potential
  security issues before they escalate.

- Scripting and Coding: LLMs possess the abilitiy to generate tailored codes, or provide assistance with the creation of code for 
  penetration testing and security assessments.

== LLM Capabilities, Black Hat ==
- Used to generate sophisticated Social Engineering scripts or Phishing campaigns much more effectively than traditional methods.

- Automatically generate exploits for targetted vulnerabilities.


** Typical Attack Chain **
1. Footprinting And Reconnaissance
2. Scanning and Enumeration
3. Vulnerability Analysis
4. Maintaining Access
5. Covering Tracks


** Cyber Kill Chain Methodology **
This provides a general guideline as to the phases of a comprehensive attack. If a singular stage of the kill chain is stopped or broken, the whole
attack fails - this specifically applies to sophisticated attacks that reach for data exfiltration, and not simple viruses. if ransomware is
delivered, files may potentially not be exfiltrated however can still be encrypted.
