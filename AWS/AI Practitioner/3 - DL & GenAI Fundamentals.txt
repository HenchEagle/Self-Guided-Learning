Input, Hidden Layer [Which can consist of many hops], and the output layer.

It finds patterns by adjusting the connections between the nodes known as weights and biases, there are primarily aquired through supervised and reinforcement training


=-=-=-= Generative AI =-=-=-=
## Pre-Training
Foundational models are typically train in whats known as self-supervised learning - This process takes vast amounts of unstructured data and creates patterns.
This self-supervised aspects takes data and transforms it using different methods. If the results vary vastly from a slgiht change to the same piece of data, the weights and biases are modified.

This step is known as pre-training and continuous pre-training. The purpose of this is to build the AIs large knowledge base and generalise accross domains.


## Optimisation
Pre-trained models are optimised using a variety of techniques like, retrievel augmented generation (RAG), prompt-engineering, and fine-tuning on task specific data.


## Evaluation
Both pre-trained models that are fine-tuned or not are evaluated using appropraite metrics, this is essential to ensure that the model meets business needs.

=-=-=-= Types of Foundational Models =-=-=-=
## LLMs
These are powerful models designed to understand and generate human-like text.

They work with tokens, which are basic units of text designed to standardise input data.
They also work with embeddings and vectors, these are kind of like coordinates on a grind, text closer to the word being analysed, are more likely to be related.


## Diffusion Models
These work with forward and reverse diffusion training,

to be able to turn pure noise or random data into coherent information.


## Multimodal
These combine different foundational models into a singla unified model.

## Generative Adversial networks
These utilise 2 neural nets competing, the generator which creates data, and t he discriminator, which tkaes a real-set of data and has to distinguish between the real set and the one generated by
the generator.

This is how deepfakes work.


=-=-=-= Methods of Optimisation / Fine-Tuning =-=-=-=
## Prompt Engineering
This is utilisied to guide and enhance the response of foundational models. Some elements of prompts are

- Instructions: What you expect the model to perform
- Context: Additional external inforamtino to guide the model
- Input Data: the input for which you want a response
- Output Indicator: The expected output format

## Fine-Tuning
This process takes a pre-trained model, and trains it utilising 2 techniques to adjust weights and biases to better align with a specific task.

- Insturction fine-tuning: Utilising examples of how to model should respond to specific tasks
- Reinforcement Learning: Rewards and penalties.

## Retrievel Augmented Generation (RAG)
A form of automated context input, through domain-relevant data such as documentation.
