** EDGE LOCATIONS **
These are smaller lightweight "footprints" of our main app caching things like images, webpages and other assets for users to get the most popular resources without 
having to retrieve them from a central location.

Consistent deployment can be achieved through IaC (infrastructure as code) specifically AWS CloudFormation

** SELECTING A REGION **
Data is not allowed to move between AWS regions, unless explicitly allowed. It can however move between AZs (availability zones), however this introduces additional costs
$0.01 / GB of data, this is where AZ-aware routing comes into play (works similarly to AS path selection)

- Compliance, data must live in region X.
- Proximity, how close you are to your customer base.
- Feature availability, not all regions have the same AWS features.
- Pricing, local costs such as TAX, energy costs etc.

# Redundant Architecture ensures availability.
This is achieved by utilising multiple availability zones, or regions.

It assists with quicker disaster recovery, latency and business continuity
