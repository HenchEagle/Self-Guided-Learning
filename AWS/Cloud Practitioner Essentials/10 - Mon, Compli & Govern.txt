** Amazon Cloudwatch **
Allows you to monitor infrastrucxture and applications through tracking of metrics

# Cloud watch alarm allows you to perform and event-driven metric based on the metric measured by cloudwatch. Such as a text message or whatevewr,

You can also centralise log management with cloudwatch logs


** AWS CloudTrail **
Because everything inside of AWS is an API call, cloudtrail automatically provides centralised logs of practically everything that happens in the cloud.


** AWS Artifact **
View compliance audits for AWS, and sign agreements with AWS based on compliance requriements.


** AWS License Manager **
Managed licenses to ensure usage doesnt exceed license agreements


** Service Control Policies (SCP) **
Allows you to centrally manage AWS organisations, users and roles access to services, resrouces and individual api calls.


** AWS Organisations **
centralised management of several AWS accounts.

Organisational units allows you to manage several accounts under a single billing plan, as well as apply service control policies based on these OUs.


### Governance

AWS Control Tower - Centralised Governance, with options to manage a whole organisation through the landing zone, or pre-configure new accounts structures to ensure best practice configs.

Service Catalog: Allows you to curate compliant, pre-configured networking ressrouces that are approved and can be deployed by approvde individuals to maintain agility, while improving governance.

License Manager: allows you to bring your own licenses, Windows AD or whatever without having to buy them from AWS.
