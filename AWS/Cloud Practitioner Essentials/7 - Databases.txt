AWS DMS (Database migration service) provides options to "lift and shift" (migrate) your on premise DBMS to the cloud.

Amazon RDS (Relational database service)
provides several database engines with additional Benefits
- Automated patching, failover, redundancy and backups.

Amazon Aurora
is around 3x faster than RDS, at a 20-30% markup with much quicker failover with auomated

** NoSQL DB Services **
Amazon DynamoDB. Excellent for super fast querying, non-relational schemas and seamless scaling through a serverless service.

Best for unstructured data like IoT, real time feeds with varying outputs or ecommerce carts.

** Amazon ElastiCache **
Store frequently queried data from a database within memory rather than on disk for near-instantaneous access.

Reduces operational overhead and can save money allowing to use smaller more economic DB instances
