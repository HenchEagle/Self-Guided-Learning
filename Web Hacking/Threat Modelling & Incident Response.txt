An effective threat modelling team requires a dedicated team including:
	- Threat Intelligence
	- Asset Identification
	- Mitigation Capabilities
	- Risk Assessment

A framework to help with threat modelling that is relevant to this day is STRIDE (Spoofing Identity, Tampering with data, Repudiation Threats, Information Disclosure, Denial of service & Elevation of priviliges)

A breach of security is known as an incident

The process of remediating the incident is known as incident response

Incidents are responded to by a team known as CSIRT (Computer Security Incident Response Team)

Incident Response usually consists of 6 phases:
	- Preparation - Do we have the resources and plans to deal with this incident
	- Identification - Has the threat and threat actor been correctly identified
	- Containment - Can the threat be secured to prevent further damage to systems and to restrict lateral movement
	- Eradication - Removing the threat
	- Recovery - Performing an evaluation of the attack and ensure the business can return to full operations
	- Lessons learned - re-evaluation of what caused the incident and how this can be prevented in the future.
